# Morning Coffee Notes

Pour-over coffee rewards patience. Use a medium-fine grind, water just off the boil (about 94C), and a slow, steady pour in gentle spirals. A 1:16 ratio of coffee to water is a reliable starting point.

Freshly roasted beans, used within two to three weeks, make the biggest difference. Store them whole, away from light and heat.
